Caring for Pet Birds

Keeping companion birds like cockatiels and parakeets brings immense happiness and energy to any home. These small birds are remarkably intelligent, highly social, and full of unique personality traits that make them wonderful family pets. To keep them thriving, owners must offer a balanced diet, a spacious cage, and plenty of daily mental stimulation.

Essential Daily Care

Taking proper care of domestic birds involves a healthy daily routine. Parrots require balanced nutrition containing seeds, formulated pellets, and fresh leafy greens to avoid vitamin deficiencies. Spending dedicated time bonding with them outside of their cage will also keep them calm, friendly, and affectionate.

Daily Routine Checklist

  • Provide fresh water daily and clean the food bowls.
  • Offer a selection of safe fresh vegetables and pellets.
  • Ensure at least one hour of supervised flight time outside the cage.
